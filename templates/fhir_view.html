Rev1 hardware measures three sensors: MAX30102 (PPG → heart rate), MAX30205 (skin temperature), and LIS331DLTR (acceleration). Only Heart rate and Body temperature map to standard vital-sign LOINC codes, so only those two appear as coded Observations below. Acceleration and raw PPG counts are shown separately as plain telemetry, not FHIR-coded vitals. SpO2, blood pressure, respiratory rate, stress/EDA, and HRV are not measured by this hardware and are never shown here — only one physical band exists, so only whichever patient's band is currently bound to it ("Bind live feed" on the hospital admin page) will show any values at all; everyone else correctly shows nothing yet.
